## Exact generation prompt

Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: photographic hero for a cold-pressed edible oils ecommerce store, Sri Varahi Traders. Create a polished landscape 1536x1024 editorial still life: three tall clear glass bottles of golden groundnut and sesame oil and pale coconut oil, simple unprinted cream paper labels, dark green caps, on warm ivory limestone beside a small coconut half, a shallow bowl of peanuts and sesame seeds. Natural warm morning side light, soft honest shadows, refined Indian pantry aesthetic, subtle olive foliage, beautiful liquid translucency. Full bottles including caps visible with generous breathing room at top and bottom, balanced centered composition suited to the image half of a split website hero and mobile cropping. No writing, no logos, no medical or certification badges, no watermark.

`public/images/oils/*.svg` are repo-native sample packaging illustrations, not photographs of the client's actual bottles. Replace them when product assets are supplied.
